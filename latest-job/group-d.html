Railway RRB Group-D (Level-1) Examination 2026 : Short Details
Post Date / Update: 07 August 2026 | ONLINEPUR
Short Information : Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the Exam City Details and Admit Card for Railway Group-D CBT-I Exam 2026. All candidates who enrolled for this recruitment can check their exam city and download the Admit Card from the links provided below on ONLINEPUR.
Important Dates Application Fee
  • Notification Date : 30 January 2026
  • Online Apply Start Date : 31 January 2026
  • Online Apply Last Date : 09 March 2026 (Extended)
  • Last Date For Fee Payment : 11 March 2026
  • Correction Date : 12 – 21 March 2026
  • CBT-I Exam Date : 03 - 21 August 2026
  • Revised Tentative Exam Schedule : 03, 06, 09, 17, 21 & 25 August 2026
  • Exam City Details : 24 July 2026 (Available Now)
  • Admit Card : 31 July 2026 (Available Now)
  • Result Date : Will Be Updated Here Soon
  • For General, OBC : ₹ 500/-
  • For SC / ST / EBC / Female / Transgender : ₹ 250/-

Refund Amount (On Appearing For CBT):
  • For General, OBC : ₹ 400/- Refunded
  • For SC / ST / EBC / Female / Transgender : ₹ 250/- Refunded

Payment Mode (Online): Debit Card, Credit Card, Internet Banking, IMPS, Cash Card / Mobile Wallet.
RRB Group-D Notification 2026 : Age Limits As On 01 January 2026
  • Minimum Age : 18 Years
  • Maximum Age : 33 Years
 RRB provides age relaxation for the Group D Various Posts in Level 1 position as per regulations.

RRB Group D Various Posts in Level 1 : Eligibility Criteria
Candidates who have completed Class 10 (High School) from a school recognized by NCVT/SCVT OR hold a National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) issued by NCVT are eligible to apply for the RRB Group D Recruitment Exam.
How To Check & Download Railway RRB Group-D CBT-I Admit Card 2026
  1. Visit the official website of your respective Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) or use the link given below on ONLINEPUR.
  2. On the homepage, click on the “RRB Group-D CBT-I Admit Card 2026” or CEN 09/2025 Exam Schedule link.
  3. Open the Exam City Notice PDF.
  4. Check the exam date, shift timings, reporting time, and important instructions carefully.
  5. Verify all the details mentioned in the notification.
  6. Download the Exam City PDF and save it for future reference.
  7. Take a printout of the admit card for reference before the examination.

As per the official schedule, the RRB Group-D CBT-I Exam 2026 will be conducted from 03 August to 21 August 2026.
Railway RRB Group-D (Level-1) Recruitment 2026 : Mode Of Selection
  1. Computer Based Test (CBT-1)
  2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
  3. Document Verification
  4. Medical Examination
