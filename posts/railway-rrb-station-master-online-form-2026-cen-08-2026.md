### **Selection Process for RRB Station Master**
The selection process for the RRB Station Master post involves the following stages:
1.  **Computer Based Test (CBT - 1):** Preliminary screening test.
2.  **Computer Based Test (CBT - 2):** Main examination for shortlisted candidates.
3.  **Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT):** Required specifically for the Station Master post.
4.  **Document Verification (DV):** Verification of all original documents.
5.  **Medical Examination:** Thorough medical fitness test as per Railway standards.
