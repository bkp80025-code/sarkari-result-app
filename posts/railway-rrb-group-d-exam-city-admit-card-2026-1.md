# Railway RRB Group D Recruitment 2026: Exam City & Admit Card Details

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has officially released the detailed notification for the recruitment of Level-1 posts under Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) 2026. This recruitment drive aims to fill a total of 22,195 vacant positions across various technical and operational departments in Indian Railways. Eligible and interested candidates can find the essential dates, fee structures, and basic highlights below to keep track of the complete examination process.

### Important Schedule

* Official Notification Date: 30 January 2026
* Online Application Start Date: 31 January 2026
* Online Application Closing Date: 09 March 2026 (Extended)
* Last Date for Application Fee Payment: 11 March 2026
* Application Form Correction Window: 12 March 2026 to 21 March 2026
* Exam City & Intimation Slip Release Date: 24 July 2026
* CBT-I Admit Card Download Date: 31 July 2026
* Revised CBT-I Exam Schedule: 03, 06, 09, 17, 21 & 25 August 2026
* Result Declaration Date: To Be Updated Soon

### Application Fee & Refund Structure

Candidates submitting their applications online were required to pay the standard examination fee based on their respective categories. A partial or full refund is provided to candidates who appear for the Computer Based Test (CBT-1):

* General / OBC: Fee ₹ 500/- | Refund Amount: ₹ 400/-
* SC / ST / EBC / Female / Transgender: Fee ₹ 250/- | Refund Amount: ₹ 250/-

Accepted Payment Modes: Net Banking, Debit Cards, Credit Cards, IMPS, and Authorized Mobile Wallets or Cash Cards.

### Age Limit (As on 01 January 2026)

* Minimum Required Age: 18 Years
* Maximum Age Limit: 33 Years
* *(Relaxation in upper age limit is applicable as per RRB Group D regulations)*

### Educational Qualification & Eligibility Criteria

* Candidates must have passed Class 10th (High School / Matriculation) from an educational board recognized by NCVT or SCVT.
* OR candidates must hold a valid National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) issued directly by NCVT.

### Mode of Selection

1. Computer Based Test (CBT-1): Objective type online examination covering General Science, Mathematics, Reasoning, and Current Affairs.
2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET): Physical endurance tests including weight lifting and running tasks.
3. Document Verification (DV): Verification of educational certificates, identity proofs, and category certificates.
4. Medical Examination: Comprehensive medical fitness checkup conducted in designated Railway Hospitals.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Download Admit Card & Exam City Intimation

1. Visit the official regional portal of your respective Railway Recruitment Board (RRB).
2. Click on the designated link titled "RRB Group-D Level-1 CBT-I Exam City Intimation / Admit Card 2026".
3. On the login screen, enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth / Password.
4. Submit the credentials to open your candidate dashboard.
5. Review your allocated Exam City, Exam Date, Exam Center Name, Shift Timings, and Reporting Time.
6. Click on the Download Admit Card option and save the PDF file.
7. Print a clean, clear copy of the Admit Card to carry to the examination hall along with valid photo identity proof.
