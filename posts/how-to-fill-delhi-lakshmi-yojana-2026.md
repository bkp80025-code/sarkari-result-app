How To Fill Delhi Lakshmi Yojana 2026

Applicants who want to apply for Delhi Lakshmi Yojana 2026 should complete the registration and application process carefully. Before submitting the application form, applicants should read the Official Notification and check all the required details.

How To Fill Delhi Lakshmi Yojana 2026

1. Visit the official Delhi Lakshmi Yojana portal.

   First, visit the official portal related to Delhi Lakshmi Yojana 2026 and carefully check the available application instructions.
2. Complete new registration.

   If you are applying for the first time, complete the new registration process by providing the required details.
3. Verify your mobile number and Aadhaar.

   Enter your mobile number and Aadhaar details wherever required and complete the verification process.
4. Enter all the required personal details.

   Fill in your personal information carefully. Make sure that the information entered in the application form is correct and matches the details in your documents.
5. Upload the necessary documents.

   Upload all the documents required for the application in the prescribed format. Before uploading, check that the documents are clear and readable.
6. Select the preferred payment option — RD/FD or CBDC.

   Select the preferred payment option from the available options, i.e. RD/FD or CBDC, as applicable.
7. Check the application form carefully.

   Before submitting the application, check all the details entered in the form. Make sure that your personal details, Aadhaar/mobile details and uploaded documents are correct.
8. Submit the application.

   After checking all the information, submit the application form through the official portal.
9. Keep the application receipt safely.

   After successful submission, keep the application receipt safely for future reference. If a printout or download option is available, save the application receipt on your device.

Important Things To Check Before Applying

\- Read the Official Notification carefully before filling out the application form.

\- Check the Last Date before submitting the application.

\- Check the Age Limit carefully.

\- Check the Education Qualification/Eligibility requirements.

\- Keep the required documents ready before starting the application.

\- Enter your details carefully to avoid mistakes.

\- Check all the information once again before final submission.

\- After submitting the form, keep the application receipt safely for future reference.

Note

छात्रो से ये अनुरोध किया जाता है की वो अपना फॉर्म भरने से पहले Official Notification को ध्यान से जरूर पढे उसके बाद ही अपना फॉर्म भरे, (Last Date, Age Limit, & Education Qualification एक बार अच्छे से जरुरु चेक करे)
