### How to Download RRB Group D Admit Card 2026

1. Visit the official website of your respective RRB zone.
2. Click on the "RRB Group-D CBT-I Admit Card / Exam City 2026" link.
3. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth / Password.
4. Check your exam date, city, shift timings, and reporting time.
5. Download and print your Admit Card for the examination hall.
